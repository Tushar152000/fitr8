---
section: Apple Health and Health Connect
---

## Can FITR8 connect to Apple Health or Health Connect?
Yes, it's free. Go to Profile, then Health apps, and tap Connect.

## What does FITR8 show from my health app?
Steps, heart rate, BMI, blood oxygen, sleep and a recovery score.

## Does FITR8 change my health data?
No. It only reads the types you allow and never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.

## Is my health data sold or used for ads?
Never. It's not sold, not used for advertising or tracking, and not sent to analytics.

## How do I disconnect and delete my health data?
Go to Profile, Health apps, and choose "Disconnect and delete synced data". Plain "Disconnect" stops syncing but keeps what's already stored. Deleting your account removes it too.

## Why isn't my health data showing?
Check that you allowed the permissions and that your phone or watch is syncing to the health app. FITR8 syncs when you open the app. On Android, health data access for new apps can take time to be enabled.
